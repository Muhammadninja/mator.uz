# Refund & Returns Policy

**Effective Date:** __.__.____

This Refund & Returns Policy applies to products purchased through the Mator mobile application and is governed by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## 1. General Conditions

Mator aims to make the return and refund process simple and convenient for buyers.

All return, replacement, and refund requests can be submitted directly to Mator through the support channels available in the mobile application.

Buyers do not need to contact individual sellers directly. Mator will coordinate the return process and inform buyers about the required steps.

Nothing in this Policy limits the mandatory consumer rights provided under the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## 2. Order Cancellation Before Delivery

Buyers may request cancellation of an order before the product has been dispatched or handed over for delivery.

If the cancellation is accepted before dispatch, the order will be cancelled and the buyer will receive a refund of the amount paid.

If the product has already been dispatched or handed over for delivery, the applicable return procedure described in this Policy will apply.

## 3. Returns of Products in Proper Condition

Mator accepts return requests for eligible products in proper condition within ten (10) calendar days from the date the buyer receives the product.

This return option applies when, for example:
- The buyer changes their mind.
- The buyer ordered the wrong spare part.
- The product does not suit the buyer's needs.

To be eligible for this type of return, the product must:

- Be unused and not installed in a vehicle.
- Not have been modified or damaged by the buyer.
- Retain its original appearance and consumer properties.
- Be returned with its original packaging, seals, labels, and accessories, where applicable.
- Be accompanied by proof of purchase.

Certain categories of products may be excluded from change-of-mind returns under the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

These exclusions do not affect the buyer's statutory rights concerning defective, damaged, incorrectly supplied, or misrepresented products.

## 4. Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Products

Buyers may request a return, replacement, repair, or refund if a product:

- Has manufacturing or other defects that were not disclosed before purchase.
- Arrives damaged.
- Is incomplete or missing components.
- Is different from the product ordered.
- Does not correspond to the description, specifications, or compatibility information provided in the mobile application.

Such requests are handled in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

Depending on the circumstances, buyers may be entitled to:

- Replacement with an equivalent product.
- Replacement with another product with an appropriate price adjustment.
- Free repair of the defect.
- A proportional reduction in the purchase price.
- Cancellation of the purchase and a refund.

These rights apply within the applicable warranty or statutory periods established by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

Where no warranty or expiry period has been established, statutory provisions concerning the applicable period for presenting claims shall apply.

## 5. Vehicle Compatibility and Spare Parts

Buyers are encouraged to verify product compatibility with their vehicle before placing an order.

If a product does not match the vehicle compatibility information or specifications presented in its product listing, the buyer may submit a return or refund request under Section 4 of this Policy.

If the buyer independently selects an incorrect spare part despite accurate product information, the request will be handled under the return conditions described in Section 3.

For automotive spare parts that have already been installed or used, defect-related claims will be assessed according to applicable law and the available evidence.

Installation alone does not automatically eliminate statutory consumer rights. However, damage caused by incorrect installation, misuse, unauthorized modification, or normal wear may affect the outcome of a claim.

## 6. How to Request a Return or Refund

To submit a return or refund request:

1. Contact Mator through the support channels available in the mobile application.
2. Provide your order number and identify the product you wish to return.
3. Explain the reason for the return or refund request.
4. Provide photographs or videos of the product, where relevant and available.
5. Follow the return instructions provided by Mator.

Mator will review the request and inform the buyer whether additional information or a product inspection is required.

Buyers should not send products to an unspecified address without first receiving return instructions from Mator.

## 7. Return of Products

Once a return request has been accepted, Mator will inform the buyer about the applicable return method and destination.

For products that are defective, damaged, incorrectly supplied, or materially different from their description, the buyer will not be charged return delivery costs under this Policy.

For eligible returns made because the buyer changed their mind or selected the wrong product, the buyer is responsible for the return delivery costs, unless otherwise required by applicable law.

Returned products must be properly packaged to prevent additional damage during transportation.

## 8. Refund Processing

Once a refund has been approved and the purchase agreement has been terminated, Mator will initiate the refund within ten (10) calendar days.

Refunds will be issued using the same payment method originally used for the purchase, unless another method is agreed between the parties in accordance with applicable law.

The time required for the funds to appear in the buyer's account may depend on the bank or payment service provider.

Where applicable, the refund will include the amount paid for the returned product and any delivery charges refundable under applicable law or the circumstances of the order.

Mator will provide confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

## 9. Important Information

- Buyers should inspect products upon receipt and report any visible damage or discrepancies as soon as reasonably possible.
- Opening product packaging for inspection does not by itself eliminate statutory rights relating to defective or incorrectly described products.
- Products damaged through misuse, unauthorized modification, or incorrect installation may not qualify for a defect-related refund.
- A change-of-mind return does not replace or restrict the buyer's rights concerning defective or non-conforming products.

## 10. Consumer Rights

This Policy is governed by the applicable laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including the Law on Protection of Consumer Rights and the Law on Electronic Commerce.

Nothing in this Policy shall be interpreted as excluding, restricting, or waiving mandatory consumer rights or legal remedies.

In the event of any conflict between this Policy and mandatory provisions of applicable legislation, the applicable legislation shall prevail.

## 11. Contact Information

For any questions or assistance regarding cancellations, returns, replacements, or refunds, buyers may contact Mator through the support channels provided in the Mator mobile application.
