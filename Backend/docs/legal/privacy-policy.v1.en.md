## 1. What This Policy Regulates

1.1. This Policy describes:

- what personal data may be processed;
- purposes for which data is used;
- processing procedures;
- potential recipients of data;
- security enforcement measures;
- how users can exercise their rights.

1.2. The Policy applies to personal data received by Mator directly from the User, automatically during mobile app usage, or from transaction participants when provided by law and necessary for service delivery.

## 2. Data We Process

Depending on features used, Mator may process the following data categories:

### 2.1. Account Data

- mobile phone number;
- user identifier;
- first and last name (if provided);
- display name (if provided);
- email address (if provided);
- profile photo (if uploaded);
- interface language;
- account settings, including notification settings.

### 2.2. Order Data

- details of ordered Goods and quantities;
- Order cost and item breakdown;
- delivery method;
- contact phone number for Order communication;
- Order history;
- Order statuses and history of changes.

### 2.3. Delivery Addresses

- address label defined by User;
- region, district, street, and full text address;
- geographic coordinates (if saved).

### 2.4. Vehicle Data ("Garage")

- make, model, year, engine type, and technical specifications;
- Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) (if provided);
- data provided during parts selection requests, including free text input.

### 2.5. Service Appointment Data

If using appointment features for service providers (e.g., auto repair shops), details regarding the selected service provider, date, time, and associated vehicle are processed.

### 2.6. Device Data

- device model;
- operating system version;
- app version;
- device language and time zone;
- technical push notification delivery identifiers;
- app installation ID;
- date of last activity.

### 2.7. Consent Logging Data

When accepting the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Personal Data Consent, the following are logged:

- date and time of acceptance;
- document version accepted;
- interface language;
- IP address and client application request metadata.

This data is used exclusively to prove the legally binding fact of consent provision.

### 2.8. System and Error Logs

To maintain Platform security and operation, technical logs containing request timestamp, user ID, and technical error nature are processed.

### 2.9. Payment Data

When paying for Orders via connected payment services, processing data is transferred to the respective payment organization.

Mator does NOT receive or store bank card credentials: entry occurs directly on the payment processor side. Mator processes payment status, amount, timestamp, and transaction IDs assigned by the payment provider.

10.1. Mator stores personal data as long as necessary to achieve processing goals, satisfy legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain accounting records.

10.3. Certain records may be retained post account deletion if required for legal compliance, audit/tax records, dispute resolution, or enforcement of rights.

10.4. Scope of retained data and anonymization procedures are specified in Section 15.

## 11. Security

11.1. Mator implements technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

11.2. Data access is restricted to authorized systems and personnel on a need-to-know basis.

11.3. Despite security measures, no Internet transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee 100% absolute security.

## 12. Security Incidents

12.1. An information security incident is any event causing or threatening unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of personal data.

12.2. Mator maintains an internal incident response protocol covering identification, containment, risk assessment, impact remediation, documentation, and prevention.

12.3. If an incident affects User personal data and notification is required by law, Mator notifies the competent authority within statutory deadlines.

12.4. If an incident poses risks to User rights and interests, Mator notifies affected Users via app alerts, SMS, or email as required by law.

12.5. User notifications under clause 12.4 detail incident nature, affected data categories, potential impact, remediation steps, recommendations, and contact info.

12.6. Mator may delay notifications if necessary to prevent further damage or upon official authority order, within limits permitted by law.

12.7. Users can report suspected security issues or vulnerabilities using contacts in Section 18.

13.1. Certain cloud, AI, push, and technical services engaged by Mator process data using infrastructure located outside the Republic of Uzbekistan.

13.1.1. Specifically, profile photos may be sent to foreign image storage providers, selection queries to AI systems providers, and push tokens to notification delivery providers.

13.1.2. Provider infrastructure locations may change over time; regardless of location, Mator ensures compliance with clauses 13.4 and 13.5.

13.2. Uzbekistan legislation mandates local storage within the Republic of Uzbekistan for specific data categories (e.g., biometric, genetic, telecom operator user data). Such data is processed in full compliance with local storage mandates.

13.3. Mator does NOT process biometric or genetic data within Platform functions.

13.4. Other personal data categories may be processed internationally where statutory grounds exist (e.g., adequate protection level in destination state, standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules, international standards).

13.5. Mator ensures valid legal grounds and contractual coverage with all international service providers processing data outside Uzbekistan.

13.6. Provider categories are detailed in Section 6.

## 15. Consent Revocation and Account Deletion

15.1. Users may revoke consent where processing is consent-based in accordance with legal procedures.

15.2. Revocation does not affect processing legality prior to revocation.

15.3. Revocation may limit availability of specific Platform features relying on such processing.

15.4. Users can initiate account deletion within the application interface.

15.5. Account deletion permanently removes: delivery addresses, garage vehicle data, cart items, service bookings, notification settings, device tokens, AI chat histories, auth credentials/external links, active sessions, and profile photos.

15.6. Account identifiers (name, email, phone, display name) are anonymized/cleared, making the account inaccessible.

15.7. Retained in anonymized/restricted form: Order records, payments, status history (for tax/accounting compliance; contact phone and delivery link deleted); consent records (version, timestamp, document ID retained; IP address and app metadata deleted).

15.8. Technical log entries are deleted according to standard log retention schedules (clause 9.2), not instantly upon account deletion.

15.9. Mator takes reasonable measures to notify third-party providers/Sellers to cease processing data transferred prior to account deletion, except where retention is legally required.
