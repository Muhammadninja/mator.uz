manufacturer/distributor and used only for the corresponding generic model rows.
They do not establish trim-specific fitment or a generation mapping.

catalog identities and non-overlapping year boundaries.

## World production years (2026-11-16, migration `20261124000000`)

Owner decision: a Garage year is accepted when the model was **built anywhere** in
that year, because many cars in Uzbekistan are imports. `year` stays the build
year. An empty end means the model is still produced and is accepted up to next
year. The migration fills only rows whose two bounds are both empty.
