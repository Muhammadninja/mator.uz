# Vehicle image discovery year sources

Accessed 2026-11-15. Ranges below are Uzbekistan production years stated by the
manufacturer/distributor and used only for the corresponding generic model rows.
They do not establish trim-specific fitment or a generation mapping.

| Make/model row | Range | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chevrolet `chevrolet-matiz` | 2001–2018 | [UzAuto Motors: Matiz production end](https://uzautomotors.com/public/articles/gm_uzbekistan_snimaet_s_proizvodstvo_chevrolet_matiz) states assembly began in August 2001 and ended from July 2018. |
| Chevrolet `orlando` | 2014–2018 | [Chevrolet Uzbekistan history](https://chevrolet.uz/index.php/page/istoriya-chevrolet) states production began in March 2014; [UzAuto Motors: Orlando production end](https://uzautomotors.com/articles/gm_uzbekistan_snimaet_s_proizvodstvo_chevrolet_orlando) states assembly ends in Q1 2018. |

All other missing year ranges, Cobalt/Malibu generation selection, and the three
Nexia overlaps remain unresolved until reliable evidence establishes exact
catalog identities and non-overlapping year boundaries.
